About ANTROTHON'27

Guided by the foundational ethos "Think Human. Innovate for Society," ANTROTHON'27 fuses technical mastery with social empathy.

ANTROTHON’27 is a 24-hour inter-college hackathon organized by the Patrician Institute Innovation Cell (PIIC) of Patrician College of Arts and Science. The event aims to bring together student innovators, creative thinkers, and aspiring entrepreneurs from different colleges to develop innovative, technology-driven solutions to real-world societal challenges.

Guided by the tagline “Think Human. Innovate for Society,” ANTROTHON’27 emphasizes human-centred innovation, collaboration, creativity, and responsible technology. Participants are encouraged to identify meaningful societal needs, apply design thinking and emerging technologies, and develop practical prototypes that address the needs of individuals and communities.

► Key Objectives

  • ▸ Encourage innovation and creative solution engineering among college students.
  • ▸ Promote the development of technology-driven solutions to real-world societal challenges.
  • ▸ Foster interdisciplinary collaboration and teamwork.
  • ▸ Encourage the responsible application of artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.
  • ▸ Promote awareness of human rights, accessibility, healthcare, education, and sustainability.
  • ▸ Support entrepreneurial thinking and scalable social-impact prototypes.

► Expected Outcomes

ANTROTHON’27 aims to inspire students to transform ideas into functional prototypes that address meaningful societal challenges. The event seeks to encourage responsible innovation, strengthen technical and entrepreneurial skills, promote collaboration among institutions, and support solutions with measurable social impact.

Thematic Hackathon Tracks

Select your operational vector across our 3 designated thematic tracks. Teams innovate and engineer societal prototypes aligned directly with their chosen track.

TRACK // 01

Human Safety, Rights & Empowerment

Focuses on protecting human dignity, promoting safety and equality, improving accessibility, and empowering individuals and underserved communities.

  • Personal & Public Safety Applications
  • Women's & Children's Safety Ecosystems
  • Assistive Technologies for Persons with Disabilities
  • Digital Rights, Data Privacy & Legal Awareness
  • Community Hazard & Incident Reporting
  • Skill-development & Employment for Underserved Populations
TRACK // 02

Health, Education & Digital Innovation

Leveraging technology to improve healthcare accessibility, educational experiences, and the delivery of essential human services across all demographics.

  • AI-Assisted Personalized Learning & Mentorship
  • Affordable Digital Healthcare & Diagnostics
  • Preventive Health, Mental Wellness & Student Wellbeing
  • Multilingual & Vernacular Educational Tools
  • Smart Campus & Institutional Governance
  • Offline-First Tech for Low-Connectivity Communities
TRACK // 03

Sustainability, Smart Communities & Social Entrepreneurship

Challenging builders to create solutions that promote environmental sustainability, efficient resource utilization, resilient infrastructure, and inclusive economic growth.

  • Smart Waste Management & Circular Recycling
  • Water & Energy Conservation Monitoring
  • Climate Resilience & Sustainable Agriculture
  • Green Mobility & Urban Transit Efficiency
  • Community Marketplaces & Local Livelihoods
  • Social Entrepreneurship Platforms with Measurable Impact

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct intel on regulations, eligibility, fee structures, and technical protocols.

Teams must consist of 4 to 6 members (1 Team Leader + 3 to 5 additional members). Inter-disciplinary teams (e.g., Computer Science + Design + Business/Humanities) are highly encouraged!
ANTROTHON'27 features 3 designated thematic tracks: Track 1: Human Safety, Rights & Empowerment, Track 2: Health, Education & Digital Innovation, and Track 3: Sustainability, Smart Communities & Social Entrepreneurship. Teams select the track that best aligns with their project concept and submit their innovation directly under that track.
Stage 1: When your team applies, you pay an initial registration fee of ₹300 per team via the secure Razorpay gateway.
Stage 2: On 27 December 2026, 45 finalist teams are selected. Only those 45 teams pay the finalist participation fee of ₹350 per head to attend the on-site 24-hour hackathon.
Students enrolled in any recognized undergraduate or postgraduate collegiate degree program (Engineering, Arts, Science, Commerce, Management) are eligible. Valid college identity cards are mandatory during physical check-in.
Finalist teams arrive at Patrician College of Arts & Science campus. You will have 24 hours non-stop access to high-speed internet, power points, sleeping zones, meals, and mentorship. At the 24th hour, code freezes and live prototype pitching begins.
